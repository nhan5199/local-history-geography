# Demo assets and sources

`alma.jpg` is the equirectangular sample panorama from [Pannellum's simple example](https://pannellum.org/documentation/examples/simple-example/), by Matthew Petroff. It is distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The image is unmodified.

`tracemonkey.pdf` is the unchanged [Mozilla PDF.js test PDF](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/blob/master/test/pdfs/tracemonkey.pdf), *Trace-based Just-in-Time Type Specialization for Dynamic Languages* by Andreas Gal et al. Its original author and publication notices are preserved in the PDF. It is used solely as a clearly labeled technical viewer demo, not school curriculum.

`pdf.worker.min.mjs` is copied from the pinned `pdfjs-dist` 6.4.299 package. PDF.js is licensed under [Apache 2.0](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/blob/master/LICENSE); the worker retains its embedded notice. When updating that dependency, update the worker file to the same version.
